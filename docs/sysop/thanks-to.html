Thanks to

Spitfire-X has its own story, but it owes a great deal to the people and projects that came before it.

Mike Woltz and the original SPITFIRE

Thank you to Mike Woltz of Buffalo Creek Software, creator of the original SPITFIRE Bulletin Board System. SPITFIRE is the inspiration behind Spitfire-X. The familiar layout of our README also pays tribute to his original documentation.

Digital Man and Synchronet

Thank you to Rob Swindell (Digital Man) and the Synchronet contributors for making their work available. SFX's separate file and QWK transfer helpers are adapted from SEXYZ. Selected DOS Door support is adapted from DOSXTRN and SBBSEXEC, with the SFX names SFXDOOR and SFXEXEC.

Deuce and DeuceSSH

Thank you to Stephen Hurd (Deuce) for DeuceSSH, the SSH library used by Spitfire-X, and for his contributions to the Synchronet code on which our helpers are based.

My X-League Discord friends

Thank you to my X-League Discord friends, Codefenix, Stingray and Alex, who I can always turn to when I have questions.

The other projects behind SFX

Thanks also to the people behind SQLite, OpenSSL, libsodium, libarchive, zlib, curl, cpp-httplib, nlohmann JSON, Howard Hinnant's date library, and the other libraries and data listed in our third-party notices. Thanks to the ClamAV team at Cisco Talos for the antivirus tools included with the Linux package.

Thank you to OpenAI for Codex, which has helped with the planning, coding, testing and documentation of Spitfire-X.

BinkD and the BBS community

Thanks to the BinkD developers and contributors for their FTN mailer, which SFX can work with through its External BinkD option. Stock BinkD is installed and managed separately; it is not bundled with SFX. And thank you to the sysops and developers who keep BBSing alive.
